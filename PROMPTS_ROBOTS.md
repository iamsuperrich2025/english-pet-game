## 🤖 หุ่นยนต์ 10 แบบ

**1. robot_01 — แดงเพลิง หอกพลาสมา (Crimson Lancer)**
```
Crimson red and gunmetal-black armor with glowing red energy veins. Signature weapon: a long glowing plasma lance/spear held in the right hand with a blade of red energy. Sharp aggressive silhouette, forward-charging stance.
```

**2. robot_02 — ฟ้าคราม ปืนพลาสมาคู่ (Azure Twin-Cannon)**
```
Azure blue and brushed silver armor with cyan-blue glow. Signature weapon: twin shoulder-mounted plasma cannons with glowing blue muzzles. Broad, sturdy, artillery-platform build.
```

**3. robot_03 — หยกเขียว ชุดจรวด (Jade Missile)**
```
Jade green and olive-grey armor. Signature weapon: a large back-mounted missile pod rack with many missile tips visible over the shoulders. Bulky, heavily armored, siege look.
```

**4. robot_04 — ทองอร่าม เรลกันซุ่มยิง (Golden Railgun)**
```
Royal gold and ivory-white armor with fine trim. Signature weapon: an extra-long railgun sniper rifle braced over the shoulder, faint electric charging glow at the barrel. Slim, tall, precise marksman silhouette.
```

**5. robot_05 — ม่วงสายฟ้า หมัดไฟฟ้า (Violet Arc-Fist)**
```
Deep violet and electric-cyan armor. Signature weapon: oversized electric gauntlet fists crackling with cyan lightning arcs. Brawler stance, big fists, glowing knuckle cores.
```

**6. robot_06 — ส้มเพลิง พ่นไฟ (Ember Flamer)**
```
Burnt orange and charcoal-black armor with heat-glow accents. Signature weapon: an arm-mounted flame projector with a lit pilot flame, plus fuel tanks on the back. Rugged, industrial, menacing.
```

**7. robot_07 — เงินคม จานเลื่อยหมุน (Silver Sawblade)**
```
Polished silver and warning-red armor. Signature weapon: large spinning circular saw blades mounted on both forearms. Lean, fast, melee-assault silhouette.
```

**8. robot_08 — เหลืองอำพัน เกาส์ช็อตกัน (Amber Gauss)**
```
Amber yellow and slate-grey armor. Signature weapon: a wide double-barrel gauss shotgun held in both hands, thick barrels with a faint magnetic glow. Stocky, heavy-trooper build.
```

**9. robot_09 — ครามลึก ลำแสงไอออน (Indigo Ion)**
```
Indigo and white armor with a bright core. Signature weapon: a glowing ion-beam emitter built into the chest plus a focusing rifle held in hand. Sleek, futuristic, energy-caster look.
```

**10. robot_10 — ขาวน้ำแข็ง ปืนเยือกแข็ง (Frost Vanguard)**
```
Arctic white and ice-blue armor with frosted edges. Signature weapon: a frost cannon emitting a cloud of ice crystals, with frost vents on the shoulders. Cold, knightly, guardian silhouette.
```
